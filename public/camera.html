Score darts with cameras

Two cameras beside the board watch each dart go in, and the practice pages enter it for you, as if you had tapped where it landed. Set the cameras up once here: choose them, then show each one where the board is by tapping five points on its picture. The setup is kept in this browser, on this computer.

Mount the cameras close to the board, one each side, where they will stay. If a camera moves, calibrate it again.

1. Choose the cameras

The browser will ask to use your cameras. Nothing leaves this computer: the pictures are only looked at here, in the page.

Using OBS? While OBS is open it has the cameras, and the browser can't use them. Either close OBS, or choose "OBS Virtual Camera" for both cameras and calibrate each half of its picture (one board each side).

2. Show each camera where the board is

    Press on the picture, slide until the cross in the magnifier sits exactly on the spot, and let go. Drag a dot to move it. Take the board out of the picture? Use "New picture" with the darts out of the board.

    3. Try it

    Start watching, take the darts out of the board, and throw. Each dart shows on the board below. If one is wrong, tap the board where it really is: that is kept with the test pictures, which help tune it for your board.
