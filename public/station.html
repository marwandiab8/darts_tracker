Score with these cameras from any computer

Open this page on the computer the cameras are plugged into, and leave it open. It watches the board, reads every dart here, and sends it to your games on any computer or tablet where you are signed in with the same account (turn "Camera" on there). Calibrate the cameras from any computer on the Cameras page: it gets pictures from this station.

Checking…

This computer's cameras

The browser will ask to use the cameras. The pictures stay on this computer, apart from a still of the board when you calibrate from another computer.

Using OBS? While OBS is open it holds the cameras: choose its virtual camera ("OBS Virtual Camera", or "OBS") for both, and calibrate each half of its picture.

Not running

Keep this page open (it can be behind other windows). The computer must not go to sleep while you play.

    Learning from your games

    Every dart the cameras put into a game is kept here with its pictures once its visit is over: a dart you left in was read right, and one you corrected (Undo and tap, or Edit my last visit) shows where it really landed. Every few corrections the station reads them all again with other settings, and uses settings that read clearly more of them right.
