How it works
  • Finish every double, D1 to D20 and then the bull. Each try is up to 3 darts: hit on dart 1, 2 or 3, or a miss.
  • A missed double stays open. After the bull, play goes back round to the earliest open double until all 21 are done.
  • The clock counts active time only: pause it for breaks.
  • Kept throwing at one double because it felt close? Log it under Stayed on this double. Missed a run of doubles, then hit one? Use Hit a later double.
  • With the cameras on, just throw: each visit is one try at the double you're on. A dart in it is a hit on that dart; pull the visit's other darts out (they don't count), and the next double starts with 3 darts. Three darts without a hit are a miss. If you stay on a double you just missed, the next darts in that number count for it, and a hit there is logged as "stayed, hit on dart 4" (or 5, 6...). Darts pulled out before the try was over count the ones the cameras didn't see as misses.
  • Board mode is a full-screen scoreboard to read from the oche. A wrong read: tap the dart, on the board or its box, and pick what it was. Undo goes back up to 10 steps.
