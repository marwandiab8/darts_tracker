The best all-round practice round

One round tests scoring, trebles and doubles, and always scores out of , so you can see exactly how you're improving. It takes about 10-15 minutes and 57 darts.

How it works
  1. Part 1 - Shanghai 10 to 15: three darts at each number. A single scores the number, a double twice it, a treble three times. Hit a single, a double and a treble of the same number in one visit (a Shanghai) for a +100 bonus.
  2. Part 2 - Doubles: one dart at each double, D1 to D20, then one at the bull. Every hit scores 50.
  3. Part 3 - Shanghai 15 to 20: the same as Part 1.
  4. Throw each dart, then tap what it hit. The keys only offer what that target can score. "Undo" takes back the last dart.
